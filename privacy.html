Lookie Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Lookie is a video editor that works on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers that receive your videos, and we don't collect, sell or share personal information.

Your videos, photos and projects

Everything you import, edit and export stays on your device. Projects are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Exported videos are only shared where you choose to send them (for example Photos, Files or another app from the share sheet).

Camera, microphone and photos

  • Camera and microphone are used only while you are recording inside Lookie.
  • Photos: Lookie only sees the photos and videos you pick, and only adds an export to your library when you choose "Save Video" or "Save Image".

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Things that use the internet

A few optional features download things or look things up. When they do, your device contacts these services directly, and like any website they can see standard connection details such as your IP address. We don't receive any of it.

  • Auto captions and AI voiceover download their speech models once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and the jsDelivr CDN. After that they run fully on your device; your audio and text are never sent anywhere.
  • Lookie AI help: on the phone and on the website, the question you type into Lookie AI is sent to OVHcloud (an EU cloud provider that doesn't train on your data) to get an answer. Nothing else from your project is sent. In the desktop app, Lookie AI runs on your computer.
  • Apple Music previews: when you search for a song, your search words are sent to Apple's iTunes Search service to get results and 30-second previews.

Purchases

Lookie Premium is sold as an App Store subscription. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active and when it renews, so it can unlock Premium on your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Lookie has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Because Lookie doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect any from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
